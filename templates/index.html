Tweet Emotion Labeling Study

What this is. A CSE 594 (Human-AI Interaction) course assignment. We are collecting human emotion labels for real tweets. It takes about 2 minutes.

What you will do. You will see {{ n }} tweets on one page. For each tweet, choose the one emotion the author is expressing.

The {{ emotions|length }} emotion categories

anger
irritation, frustration, outrage — "i feel so insulted by that reply"
fear
anxiety, worry, dread — "i feel nervous about the results tomorrow"
joy
happiness, delight, contentment — "i feel so grateful for today"
love
affection, fondness, caring toward someone — "i feel so loved by my family"
sadness
unhappiness, loneliness, disappointment — "i feel empty without her"
surprise
shock, amazement, being caught off guard — "i feel stunned it worked"

How to choose

  • Label the emotion the author is expressing, not how the tweet makes you feel.
  • There is no scored right answer and you are not being graded — emotion is genuinely ambiguous, so pick the closest fit even when none is perfect.
  • You can change any answer until you press submit.
  • If a tweet is truly ambiguous or none of the six fit, say so in the comment box at the end — that feedback is useful to us.
